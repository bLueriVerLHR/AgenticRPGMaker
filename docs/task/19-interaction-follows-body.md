# Task 19 — Interaction follows the body: deterministic patrol + talk

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Goal** | An event's interaction resolves at the tiles its body **currently occupies** (its live entity transform), not at its authored map tile. A patrolling NPC can then be talked to deterministically — the player walks up to where the NPC actually stands and presses confirm — and the recorded task-18 workaround (quest slime authored static) can be reverted to a real patrol. |
| **Why** | Task 18 recorded an engine-semantics finding: `tryInteract()` matched the event's **authored** `event.x/y` while a patrolling entity carries the **solid collider** with it. Interaction and collision therefore used different frames of reference: the NPC's home tile stayed "talkable" while the body blocked two other tiles mid-move (and the player could stand on the vacated home tile). Combining patrol + talk on one NPC was nondeterministic, so quest content had to design around the engine (static slime; the cave bat carries the patrol demo). Owner-selected next phase (2026-08-31, after the vertical slice shipped). |
| **Approach** | **Rule (runtime, `map-scene.ts`):** the faced tile "hits" an event iff the event's 1×1 body AABB at its **live transform position** strictly overlaps the faced tile's 1×1 AABB — exactly the rule `checkStep()` (movement.ts, `aabbsOverlapStrict`) already applies to solid bodies. Consequences: (a) an at-rest NPC is interactable from exactly its tile — unchanged behavior for every static event (triggers, doors, crates); (b) a mid-move NPC is interactable from **both** tiles its body spans — precisely the tiles it currently blocks, so "can talk to" ≡ "am blocked by"; (c) the authored tile remains only the spawn/home position. **Fallback:** events without a scene entity (defensive; `SceneGraph.fromMap` always builds one) resolve at the authored tile. **Order:** events are tested in map authoring order and the first hit runs its page — unchanged first-match semantics, now documented. No core/schema change: behaviors already move entities (task 09) and collision already follows the body. **Tests (runtime):** patrolled-away NPC is talkable at its current tile and its vacated home tile is inert; a fractional mid-move body is talkable from both spanned tiles and not from a third; a real `rule-based` patrol ticked through `scene.update()` is talkable while between waypoints; static-event behavior is byte-for-byte unchanged (exact-tile match). **Sample:** the quest slime (`evt_slime`) regains a small patrol (waypoints (5,5)→(4,5), speed 1) — it blocks and guards the forest-road corridor like the text claims; the quest E2E presses Z at (6,5) with a bounded retry (the slime rests exactly on (4,5) for ≤1 tick per cycle, otherwise it always spans (5,5)); the stale "patrolling slime" comment becomes true. **Docs:** project log entry; task-18 note updated to point here. |
